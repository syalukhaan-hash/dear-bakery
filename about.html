🍰 About Dear Bakery 🍰

Founded in 2025, Dear Bakery began as a small home kitchen with a dream to bring joy through baked goods.

Dear Bakery believes that taste tell a story of warmth, tradition, and modern delight.

Every flavor is crafted with love, blending classic comfort with a fresh, elegant touch.

Our mission is simple: to make every bite memorable.

Dear Bakery - Core Values

  • High quality and fresh ingredients, sourced locally whenever possible
  • Zero artificial preservatives or flavors, while maintain the sweetness
  • Prioritize customers happiness
  • Sustainability in packaging and operations

Dear Bakery - Baking Process

Every item goes through a careful, loving process:

  1. Ingredient Selection - We carefully choose the finest ingredients from local suppliers
  2. Hand Mixing - Each batch is mixed by hand with care and attention
  3. Slow Proofing - We allow our dough to rise naturally for the best flavor
  4. Small-Batch Baking - Baked fresh in small batches throughout the day
  5. Fresh Delivery - Delivered warm to ensure maximum freshness and quality
